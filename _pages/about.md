I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Software Engineering at Auburn University, working with [Dr.Yang Zhou]([https://YOUR-ADVISOR-WEBSITE](https://eng.auburn.edu/directory/yzz0154)).

In my research, I focus on understanding how AI systems learn, adapt, and make decisions, and how these processes affect their security and reliability. I am particularly interested in uncovering vulnerabilities in AI systems and developing methods to make them more secure and robust.


## Research Interests

- **AI Security and Safety:** Machine Unlearning Security, LLM Jailbreaking, Adversarial Machine Learning.
- **Large Language Models and Autonomous Agents:** LLM Safety, Agent Security, Self-Evolving Agents.
- **Machine Learning and Model Adaptation:** Unrewarded Exploration, Federated Fine-Tuning, Adaptive Learning.
